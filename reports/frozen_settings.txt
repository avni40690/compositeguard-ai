Methods, all declared before the test evaluation:
1. Thresholding baseline: grayscale, Gaussian 5x5, CLAHE, Otsu threshold (dark and bright
   versions), opening 3 / closing 7, minimum area 30. Image score = total region area
   (features area_dark and area_bright). Cutoffs chosen on the train split by balanced accuracy.
2. Edge density (primary classical method): Canny edges on the CLAHE image; image score =
   share of edge pixels. Also sharpness (Laplacian variance). Cutoffs chosen on train.
3. CNN normal: ResNet18, pretrained, fine-tuned 5 epochs, Adam 1e-4, best epoch chosen by
   validation accuracy (models/resnet18_best.pt). Decisions by highest probability.
4. CNN neutral (the declared PRIMARY model): same training, grayscale input with per-image
   standardization (models/resnet18_neutral.pt).

Metrics: AUC with 95% bootstrap range (200 resamples), balanced accuracy, precision, recall;
3-class accuracy and confusion matrix; severity table with the default rules (0.50 / 0.25 /
0.50 / 0.70, size rule off); full-pipeline inference time.

Localization: Grad-CAM region at 50% of the maximum, regions over 50% of the image are not
reported. Localization results come from the 10 validation boxes. They are not re-run on test.

Stage 33 will also use the test images for a failure gallery and a lighting-change test.
